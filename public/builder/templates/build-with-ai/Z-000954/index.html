Frequently

What services do you offer?

We provide internship placements to help students gain practical experience and enhance their skills.

Our internships are open to students and recent graduates from various educational backgrounds.

How do I apply for an internship?

You can apply through our website by submitting your resume and cover letter.

Are internships paid or unpaid?

Internship compensation varies; some positions offer stipends while others are unpaid.

What industries do you cover?

We work with a variety of industries, including technology, education, and business.

How long do internships last?

Internship durations typically range from a few weeks to several months, depending on the program.
