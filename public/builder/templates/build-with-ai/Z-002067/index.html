Frequently
asked questions

What is ENTEROMATCH?

ENTEROMATCH is a personalized probiotic recommendation website using a questionnaire to find the best products for you.

How does the questionnaire work?

The questionnaire asks 10-15 questions to understand your needs and suggest suitable probiotic products.

How long does the questionnaire take?

Completing the questionnaire typically takes about 5-10 minutes, depending on your responses.

Are the product suggestions personalized?

Yes, our suggestions are tailored based on your answers to ensure the best fit for your health.

Can I change my answers later?

Yes, you can revisit the questionnaire to update your answers and receive new product recommendations.

How do I book a consultation?

You can book a consultation through our website after receiving your personalized product suggestions.
