What events do you organize?

We organize workshops, seminars, and competitions related to electrical and electronics engineering throughout the academic year.

How can I participate in events?

Participation details are shared via email and our website. Register early to secure your spot!

Who can attend the events?

Events are open to all students, faculty, and interested individuals in the electrical and electronics engineering field.

Where can I find the event calendar?

The event calendar is available on our website, updated regularly with upcoming events and details.

How can I get involved with staff?

You can reach out to staff via email or during office hours for guidance and support.

Are events free to attend?

Most events are free; however, some may require a small fee for materials or refreshments.
