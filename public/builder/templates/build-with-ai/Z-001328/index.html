About Vedanga School

Founded in 2014 in the sacred city of Baidyanath, Vedanga School embraces the motto, 'As much as we live, we learn.' We provide a nurturing environment for students from Playgroup to Class X. Following the CBSE curriculum, our dedicated team focuses on holistic education, fostering both academic excellence and personal growth. Join us in shaping bright futures!
