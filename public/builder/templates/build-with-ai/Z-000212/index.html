About Spoorthy Concept School

At Spoorthy Concept School, we are dedicated to nurturing young minds in a supportive and innovative environment. Established in 2011, our mission is to provide quality education that fosters holistic development. Located in Kanagal, we combine traditional values with modern teaching methodologies, ensuring each student thrives academically and personally. Join us in shaping the future! We focus on nurturing the overall growth of students, balancing academics with extracurricular activities.

  • Our dedicated teachers bring expertise and passion, ensuring personalized attention for every student.

You can pay fees online through our Parent Portal using Razorpay, which supports UPI, cards, and net banking.

We offer online classes via Google Meet, allowing students to join classes easily and access recorded sessions.

How do I access the Parent Portal?

You can access the Parent Portal from our Home Page by clicking on the Parent Login button.

What facilities does the school provide?

The school offers modern facilities, digital learning resources, safe transport, and a nurturing environment for students.

How can I contact the school?

You can contact the school through our website's contact form or by calling the provided phone number.
