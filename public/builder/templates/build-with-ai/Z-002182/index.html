What services do you offer?

We offer venue selection, décor design, catering, and full event coordination for weddings and special events.

How do I start planning my wedding?

Contact us to schedule a consultation. We’ll discuss your vision and begin planning your dream wedding.

Can you work with any budget?

Yes, we tailor our services to fit various budgets while ensuring a memorable and elegant experience.

Do you provide vendor recommendations?

Absolutely! We have a trusted network of vendors to recommend for every aspect of your event.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 6-12 months in advance to secure your preferred date and venue.

Our cancellation policy varies by contract. Please contact us for specific details regarding your event.
