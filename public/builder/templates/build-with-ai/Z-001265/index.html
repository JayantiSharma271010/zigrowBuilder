What are the cafe's opening hours?

CHILLFLIX is open daily until 11:30 PM for your convenience.

Do you offer delivery services?

Yes, we provide no-contact delivery options for your safety and convenience.

Is there a drive-through available?

Absolutely! We have a drive-through service for quick and easy pickups.

Can I make a reservation?

Currently, we do not accept reservations; seating is first-come, first-served.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept NFC, UPI, and card payments for your convenience.

Are there vegetarian options on the menu?

Yes, our menu includes a variety of delicious vegetarian options.
