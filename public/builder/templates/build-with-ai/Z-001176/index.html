Frequently
asked questions

What is Zira?

Zira is an online shopping app for beauty, fashion, and lifestyle products, offering a seamless shopping experience.

How do I create an account?

Simply download the Zira app, click on 'Sign Up', and follow the prompts to create your account.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including credit/debit cards, digital wallets, and cash on delivery for your convenience.

Can I return my order?

Yes, you can return items within 7 days of delivery, provided they are unused and in original packaging.

How do I track my order?

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking link via email or SMS to monitor its progress.

Is there a customer support team?

Absolutely! Our customer support team is available 24/7 to assist you with any inquiries or issues.
