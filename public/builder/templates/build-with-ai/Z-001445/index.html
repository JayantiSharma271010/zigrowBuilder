Frequently
asked questions

What services does Divyam Hospital offer?

Divyam Hospital provides pharmacy, laboratory, sonography, daycare, NICU, ICU, operation theatre, and dental services.

How can I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by calling us at 9413912974 or visiting our website.

Is there an ambulance service available?

Yes, we offer ambulance services for emergencies and patient transport.

What are the visiting hours?

Visiting hours vary; please check with the hospital for specific timings.

Do you have a pediatric department?

Yes, our pediatric department is led by Dr. MG Choudhary, specializing in children's health.

Where is Divyam Hospital located?

Divyam Hospital is located at 10A Mahabalipuram, near Sampat Place, Nokha Road, Gangashahar, Bikaner.
