Frequently
asked questions

What is a Confidence Fit T-shirt?

It's a comfortable t-shirt designed for men, avoiding tight fits around the stomach.

What sizes are available?

Currently, we offer M, L, and XL sizes to minimize returns.

What fabric is used?

Our t-shirts are made from 100% cotton or a cotton-poly blend for comfort and durability.

How do I care for my t-shirt?

Wash in cold water, tumble dry low, and avoid heavy ironing to maintain fit.

How can I order?

You can place an order via WhatsApp or Instagram; just send us a message!

What is the return policy?

We accept returns within 7 days if the product is unworn and in original condition.
