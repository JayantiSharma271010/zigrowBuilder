Dance Forms We Teach

Explore our diverse offerings in Indian classical dance styles.

Bharatanatyam is one of the oldest classical dance forms from South India. It combines intricate footwork, expressive hand gestures, and facial expressions to tell stories from Indian mythology. Our classes focus on the traditional techniques, rhythms, and the spiritual essence of Bharatanatyam. Students will learn the basic postures, movements, and the significance of each gesture, enabling them to perform with grace and confidence. Whether you are a beginner or looking to refine your skills, our experienced instructors will guide you through every step of this beautiful art form.

Bharatanatyam Classical Dance South India Art Culture

Kathak is a North Indian classical dance that narrates stories through intricate footwork, spins, and expressive gestures. Our Kathak classes emphasize storytelling, rhythm, and the emotional expression that this dance form embodies. Students will learn various techniques, including the famous 'chakkars' (spins) and 'tatkaar' (footwork), while also understanding the historical context of the dance. Our instructors, well-versed in the nuances of Kathak, create a nurturing environment for students to explore their creativity and develop their dance skills.

Kathak North India Dance Storytelling Rhythm Expression

Odissi is a classical dance form that originated in the temples of Odisha. It is characterized by its graceful movements, intricate footwork, and expressive poses. Our Odissi classes focus on the 'tribhangi' posture and the fluidity of movements, which reflect the beauty of the female form. Students will learn about the rich cultural heritage of Odissi, including its music and costumes. Our experienced instructors will guide you through the various 'mudras' (hand gestures) and 'abhinaya' (expressive acting), helping you to connect deeply with this enchanting dance form.

Odissi Odisha Dance Grace Heritage Culture

Mohiniyattam is a graceful dance form from Kerala, known for its feminine style and expressive storytelling. It combines elements of both dance and drama, often depicting themes of love and devotion. Our classes focus on the intricate footwork and the subtle expressions that define Mohiniyattam. Students will learn the significance of each movement and gesture, immersing themselves in the lyrical beauty of this dance form. Our dedicated instructors ensure a supportive environment for students to express themselves and grow as performers.

Mohiniyattam Kerala Dance Feminine Storytelling Devotion

Kuchipudi is a vibrant dance form from Andhra Pradesh, known for its unique combination of dance and drama. It often involves storytelling through expressive movements and intricate footwork. Our Kuchipudi classes emphasize the importance of rhythm and expression, allowing students to embody the characters they portray. Students will learn various traditional pieces and techniques, including the famous 'Tarangam' performance, where dancers perform on the edges of a plate. Our instructors are passionate about sharing this dynamic art form and nurturing each student's talent.

Our Dance Fitness classes blend traditional dance forms with modern fitness techniques to create a fun and engaging workout. These classes are designed for all ages and skill levels, focusing on improving flexibility, strength, and coordination while enjoying the rhythm of dance. Students will experience a variety of dance styles, incorporating elements from Bharatanatyam, Kathak, and more, making fitness enjoyable and accessible. Our enthusiastic instructors motivate participants to stay active and healthy while exploring their passion for dance.
