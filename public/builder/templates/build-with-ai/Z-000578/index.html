Frequently
asked questions

What types of sarees do you sell?

We offer a variety of sarees, including raw mango, Banaras, Mysore silks, and trendy fancy styles.

How can I place an order?

Simply browse our online collection, choose your saree, and follow the checkout process to place your order.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we provide international shipping options to ensure our sarees reach customers worldwide.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods, including credit cards, debit cards, and online payment platforms for your convenience.

How can I track my order?

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email to monitor its delivery status.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 14-day return policy for unworn sarees, ensuring customer satisfaction with every purchase.
