Frequently
asked questions

What is Students Planner?

Students Planner helps students organize their academic year by creating personalized study plans based on their schedules and subjects.

How do I upload my timetable?

You can easily upload your timetable by navigating to the upload section on our website and following the prompts.

Is it free to use?

Yes, Students Planner offers a free version with essential features to help you plan your studies effectively.

Can I customize my study plan?

Absolutely! You can adjust your study plan to fit your preferences and learning pace at any time.

What subjects can I add?

You can add any subjects relevant to your curriculum, ensuring your study plan is tailored to your academic needs.

Is my data secure?

Yes, we prioritize your privacy and use secure methods to protect your data while using Students Planner.
